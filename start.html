Start Here

Three things. Pick one.

You do not need to do all of this.

Doing one of these today is a win. The rest can wait — there is no schedule.

Step 1

Make the text bigger

Easiest one. Under a minute. If things are hard to read, this fixes that right away.

What to do
  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Accessibility
  3. Tap Display & Text Size
  4. Slide Larger Text to on
Heads up

This is the one thing Siri can't do for you — it'll only point you to the setting. You'll have to move the slider yourself. Takes about 20 seconds.

Step 2

Stop accidental taps

If your hand shakes and the phone does things you didn't mean — this is the fix.

Easiest way — just say this

“Hey Siri, turn on Touch Accommodations.”

Or do it by hand
  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Touch
  2. Tap Touch Accommodations → turn on
  3. Turn on Hold Duration
  4. Set Ignore Repeat to about 0.3 seconds

Step 3

Control the phone with your voice

The big one. Bigger effort — maybe save this for a day you feel good. But it's the one that changes the most.

After it's set up once, just say

“Hey Siri, turn on Voice Control.” — and to stop it, “turn off Voice Control.”

First-time setup (needs Wi-Fi)
  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Voice Control
  2. Turn it on — a file downloads
  3. Say “Show numbers” — then say a number to tap it
Watch: 75 seconds ▶

Or just ask someone to set it up

These are all settings, not habits. Somebody else can turn them on for you in a few minutes — you'd only need to use them afterwards. That's allowed.

And if you have the new Siri, you can simply ask it to turn most of these on for you.
